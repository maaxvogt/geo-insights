What gets checked here — and what does not

What gets analyzed is the raw HTML of the URL you enter, which is exactly what GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot receive when they fetch it. These crawlers do not run JavaScript, so content that only appears in the browser does not exist for them. That is why the rendered result is deliberately not what gets scored.

All 34 checks are rule-based and traceable: no model judges your content here, and no result drifts between two runs. The tool tells you whether your page is technically and structurally quotable. It can not tell you whether ChatGPT actually names you today — that also depends on brand, links and competition.

Alongside the page itself, /robots.txt, /llms.txt and /sitemap.xml of the same domain are fetched. No cookies, headers or credentials are ever passed on, and nothing is stored.
